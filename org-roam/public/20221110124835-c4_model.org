#+title: C4 Model

It is important to not conflate the idea of the C4 model which is a standard model for diagramming software systems with the notion of a script-based diagramming such as using [[id:35CBC978-AF9B-491D-A4D0-FE728BA540A3][diagrams]] or graphviz or some other language that compiles to graph visualizations.

Two ideas are independent but can coexist. In fact the author of the C4 model recommends that users do not use freeform illustration tools like lucid, draw.io: Vizio, Miro, PowerPoint, google drawings, etc.…

Tools like diagrams support C4 natively. At least starting out this is my personal choice for implementing for illustrations.
