#+title: Common Data Model
From [[https://github.com/microsoft/CDM#:~:text=Star%201.1k-,The%20Common%20Data%20Model%20(CDM)%20is%20a%20standard%20and%20extensible,%2C%20Opportunity%2C%20Product%2C%20etc.][this repo]].

"The Common Data Model (CDM) is a standard and extensible
collection of schemas (entities, attributes, relationships)
that represents business concepts and activities with
well-defined semantics, to facilitate data
interoperability. Examples of entities include: Account,
Contact, Lead, Opportunity, Product, etc."
