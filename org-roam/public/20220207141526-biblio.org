#+title: biblio
Efficient browsing of bibliographic references within [[id:8423A445-3117-4147-BDA0-197E6F134505][Emacs]].
Outputs [[id:288895DE-319D-4ADE-BC73-F4384F258FF0][BibTeX]], making it play nicely with the rest of the
manuscripting framework.


"biblio.el makes it easy to browse and gather bibliographic
references and publications from various sources, by
keywords or by DOI. References are automatically fetched
from well-curated sources, and formatted as BibTeX."
